Enable Microphone Access

Grant permission so you can speak your departure, destination, and bus preferences hands-free.

⚠️ Chrome Blocked Microphone Access

  1. Look at the top Chrome address bar for this tab.
  2. Click the Tune / Site controls icon (🎛️) or Lock icon on the left side of the address bar.
  3. Switch Microphone from Block to Allow.
  4. Click the "Test Microphone Again" button above!
Chrome Manifest V3 requires microphone permissions to be granted via a tab once.
